# Apple's license terms

Hangar runs Apple's iOS Simulator, which comes with Xcode, on Macs you own. Whether your use is
allowed depends on Apple's terms, not on Hangar, and Hangar makes no claim either way. Read them
before you lend simulators to people or programs outside the Mac's own user:

- the Xcode and Apple SDKs agreement, which you accept when you install Xcode and can read again
  in Xcode's About window or at [developer.apple.com/support/terms](https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/);
- the macOS software license agreement for your version of macOS, at
  [apple.com/legal/sla](https://www.apple.com/legal/sla/).

Two things in how Hangar works are worth knowing when you read them:

- Simulators run on the Mac that Hangar runs on. Hangar sends the screen and the input over the
  network; it doesn't move Apple's software anywhere else.
- Hangar doesn't offer hosted Macs or resell devices, and it isn't planned to.

If you need an answer for your company, ask your own lawyer.
